Why everyone's talking about it

Two years ago, if you mentioned Claude in an AI conversation, you'd get a polite smile. Today, it's a different story. Anthropic's share of enterprise AI spending jumped from 12% in 2023 to 40% in 2025, according to available data — overtaking OpenAI in adoption momentum. And as of this article, Anthropic's annualized revenue exceeds $47 billion, compared to $25 billion for OpenAI. 70% of Fortune 100 companies are Claude customers.

This is no longer a challenger. It's become a reference in its own right.

But numbers only tell part of the story. What I care about is understanding why Claude is attracting so much attention — and whether it's actually relevant for someone like me: an entrepreneur who uses AI every day without writing a single line of code.

Where Claude actually comes from

Anthropic was founded by former OpenAI researchers — the same company that built ChatGPT. They left with a conviction: AI needs to be built differently, with safety and ethics as structural priorities, not last-minute add-ons.

They developed what they call constitutional AI: Claude is trained to follow a set of explicit principles — something like an internal charter — that guides its responses. In practice, that means a model that refuses things less arbitrarily, explains its limits more clearly, and produces more nuanced answers on sensitive topics.

This isn't marketing. It's an architectural choice that has real, concrete effects on the quality of your conversations.

Claude isn't a single model — it's a family. As of this article, here's what's available:

  • Claude Sonnet 5 (released June 30, 2026): the default model on claude.ai, for both free and Pro accounts. This is the one you'll use first.
  • Claude Opus 5 (launched July 24, 2026): the most powerful model, described as thoughtful and proactive, reserved for paid subscribers.
  • Claude Haiku 4.5 (launched October 15, 2025): the fastest and most cost-efficient, built for high-frequency use cases where speed matters most.

Each model serves a different need. For an entrepreneur just getting started, Sonnet 5 is more than enough.

What Claude actually does better

I'm not going to tell you Claude is "better" than ChatGPT across the board. That would be wrong, and it wouldn't mean much anyway. Both are excellent. But Claude pulls ahead on specific dimensions that matter to me.

Reasoning over long documents. Claude can analyze very long texts in a single conversation, thanks to an extended context window. Concretely: you can paste in an 80-page contract, an annual report, or a thread of emails, and ask it to analyze, summarize, or compare them. Without losing the thread. Without asking you to "summarize first."

Structured writing quality. Claude produces well-organized text with clear internal logic. For writing a business proposal, structuring a strategy, or drafting a brief, that's a real advantage. The output feels less "generic AI" and more "someone who actually thought it through."

Agentic behavior. Claude Sonnet 5 is described by Anthropic as the most "agentic" Sonnet model to date. If that word doesn't mean much to you yet, I'd point you to this article on agentic workflows — the short version: Claude can chain reasoning steps autonomously, not just answer a single question.

Reliability on code. Claude Opus 4.8 (launched May 2026) is roughly four times less likely to let an error slip through its own code without flagging it. For someone using AI to automate tasks without being a developer, that difference matters: you want a tool that tells you when it's unsure, not one that hallucinates with confidence.

My takeaways

If you regularly work with long documents — contracts, reports, email archives — Claude is probably better suited than ChatGPT for that kind of task. Not because it's "smarter," but because its context window and behavior on structured text are specifically designed for it.

How to try Claude without paying

It's simple. Go to claude.ai, create an account with your email, and start. No credit card. No commitment.

The free plan gives you access to Claude Sonnet 5, the default model. It's already very capable for the vast majority of use cases: writing, analysis, brainstorming, document summarization.

What are the free plan's limits? The number of messages per day is capped, and you don't have access to the most powerful models like Opus 5. If you use Claude heavily, the Pro plan unlocks more capacity and access to additional models.

My recommendation: start with the free version. Test it on your real use cases. If you hit the limits in under a week, that's actually a good sign — it means the tool is genuinely useful to you.

One important note on memory: since July 10, 2026, Claude handles memory differently. It now works as a set of individual, categorized entries that Claude reads and updates across conversations. It's more precise than the daily summary system it used before. In practice, Claude can better remember your preferences, your context, your ongoing projects — as long as you give it that information.

To get more out of your conversations with Claude, the quality of your prompts makes all the difference. I wrote a dedicated article for non-developers on how to write a good prompt — it's probably the most useful thing to read right after creating your account.

FAQ

Is Claude really more powerful than ChatGPT?

Not across the board. Both are solid. Claude pulls ahead on specific dimensions: reasoning over long documents, structured writing, agentic behavior. ChatGPT has its own strengths, especially around multimodality and tool integrations. The right choice depends on what you actually do.

Can I use Claude to generate images or visuals?

No — as of this article, Claude doesn't generate images. It's a language model: it processes and produces text. For visuals, other tools are better suited — I cover them in my article on choosing your AI based on your needs.

What's the difference between Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku?

Simply put: Opus is the most powerful, for complex tasks and long reasoning chains. Sonnet is the performance/speed sweet spot — it's the default model you'll use day to day. Haiku is the fastest and most cost-efficient, built for high-frequency use cases where speed matters more than depth.

How do I connect Claude to my tools without writing code?

Claude integrates via platforms like Zapier or Make, which bridge claude.ai and your other tools (Gmail, Notion, Slack…) without you writing a single line of code. I walk through this approach in my article on no-code automation.

Is Claude safe to use with confidential information?

Anthropic built Claude with a strong safety philosophy, and that's part of what draws large enterprises — 70% of the Fortune 100 use it. That said, as with any cloud tool, avoid pasting ultra-sensitive data (account numbers, customer personal data) into an online interface without reading the terms of service first. Anthropic's Enterprise plan offers additional data privacy guarantees.

Is there an official Claude app?

Yes. Claude is accessible via the web at claude.ai, and Anthropic offers official mobile apps. Check your device's app store for the latest version.
