— Definition

Reasoning model, in plain words

A reasoning model takes more time to think before answering a complex task.

Champion useful slowness when precision matters.

A concrete example

To sort an email, no point. To analyze a complex plan or fix a tricky bug, it can make the difference.

Why it matters

Not every problem calls for the same depth of reasoning.

You run into it in thinking options, problem-solving models, and planning tasks.

Don't mix it up with

AI model: An AI model is the engine that reads your request and produces an answer.

Chain of thought: Chain of thought is the step-by-step reasoning a model can work through before it answers.

Common mistakes

  • Using it for everything.
  • Confusing slowness with intelligence.
  • Forgetting to give the model enough context.
